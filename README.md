🤖 AI Art Inspector — 120 Random Prompt/Challenge Mode 🎨✨
> An AI-powered artwork assessment tool featuring a 120-random-challenge system, pause functionality, and a 100-point scoring grading system. It critiques your art strictly—without generating or drawing a single stroke for you! 🚫🤖🖌️
> 
🌟 Overview
AI Art Inspector is designed for artists who want rigorous, objective evaluations of their artwork.
Unlike generative AI tools that create or touch up artwork, this app only evaluates and scores your submitted pieces. It features a robust 120-random-challenge mode, allows you to pause your progress, and grades your work strictly on a 100-point scoring scale while leaving all the drawing 100% to you! ✍️🎯
✨ Key Features
 * 🎲 120 Random Challenges: Test your skills against a curated pool of 120 randomized drawing prompts and technical constraints.
 * ⏸️ Pause & Resume: Take a break anytime! Save your current challenge state and resume whenever you're ready.
 * 💯 100-Point Scoring System: Get precise, multi-criteria grading out of 100 points covering anatomy, proportions, composition, and execution.
 * 🔍 Pure Inspection Only: Zero generative features—the AI critiques and scores, but never draws or edits for you.
 * 📊 Detailed Breakdowns: Receive actionable critiques and pointers on where you lost points so you can improve yourself.
🛠️ How It Works
 * 🎯 Accept the Challenge — Receive a randomly generated prompt from the pool of 120 challenges.
 * ⏱️ Draw & Pause — Work on your piece manually. Use the pause feature anytime you need a break.
 * 📤 Submit — Upload your finished artwork for inspection.
 * 🤖 Get Scored — The AI analyzes your work against the challenge criteria and assigns a final score out of 100 with strict, constructive feedback! 📝✨
🎯 Project Goal
To provide a challenging, gamified environment that pushes human artists to improve their fundamentals through strict evaluation and scoring, while keeping human craftsmanship completely authentic. 🎨🏆
